Your website rebuild, explained

A short, straightforward guide to what we’re doing to your website, where it will live, and why it stays completely in your hands — no matter who works on it in the future.

01 — What’s changing

Same website. New engine underneath.

Your site is being rebuilt using newer technology, but it is still your site. Same blue colours, same logo, same services, same words on the page. Visitors won’t notice anything is different — except that it opens faster.

✓

The same look

Same blues, same logo, same layout, same photos. Nothing about the design is being reinvented without your say-so.

✓

The same content

Every page, every service description and every phone number carries straight across. Your existing wording is reused, not rewritten.

✓

Faster, and it breaks less

Pages will load noticeably quicker, especially on phones. And there is far less to go wrong, because there are far fewer moving parts.

The simplest way to picture it

Think of your website as a house. We are keeping the rooms, the paint colour and the furniture exactly where they are. We are only replacing the old wiring and plumbing behind the walls, because the old wiring is slow and keeps needing repairs.

02 — Where your website will live

Moving from Hostinger to Vercel

Every website has to sit on a computer somewhere that keeps it switched on and available. That is called hosting. Today yours sits on Hostinger; after the rebuild it will sit on Vercel.

What Vercel is

A hosting service built specifically for modern websites like the one we are making. It is the same service large, well-known companies use to keep their sites online.

Why it is faster

Your site is kept ready on computers in many locations at once, so every visitor is served from one near them. Traditional shared hosting serves everyone from a single spot.

Why it is safer

WordPress runs on plugins — small add-on programs written by lots of different people. Out-of-date plugins are the most common way WordPress sites get hacked. Your new site has none at all.

03 — The important one

What if something happens to your developer?

This is exactly the right question to ask, and the honest answer is short: you keep everything. The website, its code and its accounts are yours, and any qualified web developer could pick it up and carry on. Here is why.

1

Built with standard technology

Your site is built with Next.js — one of the most widely used website technologies in the world, used by companies like Nike, Netflix and TikTok. It is a common, well-documented tool, not a custom system only one person understands.

2

The code is stored safely

The complete website is kept in GitHub — think of it as a secure vault for the code, holding a dated record of every change ever made. It is never sitting only on one person’s computer.

3

The accounts are in your name

The hosting and the code vault are owned by you, under your email. You can add or remove anyone at any time — including me. You cannot be locked out of your own website.

Put bluntly

If I disappeared tomorrow, you would hand a new developer two logins and they could be working on your website the same day. There is nothing to figure out from scratch, nothing to buy back, and nothing that only I hold a copy of.
