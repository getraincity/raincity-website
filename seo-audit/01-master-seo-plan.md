**Prepared:** 2026-09-02
**Site:** RainCity Property Maintenance — https://raincitypms.com
**Basis:** `seo-audit/00-current-state.md`

## The strategic call, up front

The competitive research (§1.4) found that the strongest ranking competitor in
this market — Shine City Pressure Washing — runs a **service × location matrix
of roughly 60–96 pages**, at ~6,500 words each, with 14 FAQs per page and 291
displayed reviews.

The obvious move is to copy that: 11 services × 9 communities = 99 pages.

**This plan deliberately does not do that, and the reason matters.**

The competitor's matrix works because each page carries real, differentiated
substance and real review proof. RainCity has neither yet — it has 6
unconfirmed testimonials and no review count it can publish. A 99-page matrix
generated in one pass from templated copy with the city name swapped is the
textbook doorway-page pattern. It is the exact risk CLAUDE.md names as the
thing this build has refused from the start, and Google's Helpful Content
system is specifically tuned to catch it. It would put 99 thin URLs on a domain
whose existing 20 commercial pages are genuinely good — and dilute them.

So the architecture plan is:

1. **Make the 20 existing pages fully connected** — the biggest uncaptured win,
   and it costs no new copy at all. Right now the service pages, the most
   valuable templates on the site, link to nothing.
2. **Deepen before widening.** Bring the existing pages to competitive depth.
3. **Build the matrix later, deliberately, in tranches** — the top 12–18
   combinations only, each with client-confirmed local substance, once review
   proof exists. Specified in §2.3 as a funded Phase-7 project, not executed
   blind now.

That sequencing is the plan's main opinion. Everything below follows from it.

## 2. Site architecture

### 2.1 Current

```
/                     hub
├── /about
├── /services         ──► 11 service pages
├── /locations        ──► 9 location pages ──► all 11 services + 2-3 neighbours
├── /blog             ──► 6 posts                        [ALL NOINDEX]
├── /contact
└── /terms, /privacy-policy                              [NOINDEX]
```

### 2.2 Target after this pass — no new routes, a complete graph

```
/services/[slug]  ──► all 9 /locations/[slug]     [NEW - closes the loop]
                  ──► 2-3 related /services/[slug] [NEW]
/blog/[slug]      ──► /services/[slug], /locations/[slug]  [NEW - link block]
```

Adding the service→location band creates **99 new internal links** pointing at
the location tier, which is currently receiving none from the service tier.
Adding related-services creates ~28 more. This is the single largest structural
change available without writing a word of unverified copy.

### 2.3 Deferred: the service × location matrix (Phase 7, funded separately)

**Not built in this pass.** When it is built, these are the conditions:

- **Tranche 1 — 12 pages only.** The 4 highest-intent services (pressure
  washing, gutter cleaning, roof cleaning, window cleaning) × the 3 largest
  markets (Surrey, Burnaby, Vancouver). URL form: `/services/[service]/[city]`.
- **Entry condition:** RainCity has ≥20 published Google reviews, so the pages
  can carry real proof.
- **Per-page bar:** ≥800 words of copy that would be *false* if the city name
  were swapped. Minimum: the local building stock, the local access constraint,
  two FAQs specific to that city's bylaws or conditions, one named local job.
- **Automated gate:** extend `check-unique.mjs` to fail the build if any pair of
  matrix pages exceeds 35% 8-gram overlap.
- **Do not proceed to Tranche 2** until Tranche 1 pages are indexed and ranking.

If the client will not supply that substance, **do not build the matrix.** Nine
excellent location pages beat 99 thin ones.

## 4. On-page and metadata plan

### 4.1 Title tag rules

- **Hard limit 60 characters.** Anything longer is truncated in the SERP.
- Pattern by page type:
  - Service: `{Service} in Greater Vancouver | RainCity` — primary keyword
    first, geo second, brand last (the part safe to lose).
  - Location: `{Service-led phrase} {City}, BC | RainCity` — never
    `Property Maintenance in {City}, BC | RainCity Property Maintenance`,
    which repeats the brand and spends 30 characters on it.
  - Blog: `{Headline} | RainCity`.
- The brand goes last, always. It is the only part that can be cut without
  losing the query match.
- Never let the layout template append a second brand — `pageMetadata` already
  emits `title.absolute`; keep it that way.

### 4.2 Meta description rules

- **Hard target 150–158 characters.** Under 120 wastes the space; over 160 is
  cut mid-clause.
- Must contain: the primary keyword, the geography, and one concrete
  differentiator. Not three clauses of service list.
- **The phone number does not belong in a meta description.** It is at the end
  of the homepage's current 270-character description, which means it is the
  first thing cut. The `tel:` link on the page and the LocalBusiness
  `telephone` field already serve that job.
- Every description unique. Currently true — keep it true.

### 4.3 Header hierarchy rules

- Exactly one `<h1>` per page. Currently true on all 37 public routes.
- `<h2>` for section headings; `<h3>` for cards and FAQ questions; `<h4>` only
  inside the FAQ accordion. No skipped levels.
- The `<h1>` should not be the bare service or city name where a phrase would
  carry the keyword — but **not at the cost of the design**. `ServiceHero`
  renders `detail.heading`, which is already a phrase. Location pages render
  the bare city name as `<h1>`, which is correct for a page whose entire
  subject is that city.
- **Never put `max-w-prose` on a heading** — documented trap in
  `components/blog/PostColumn.tsx`.

### 4.4 Image alt text standard

Already met, and the standard is worth writing down because it is unusually
good here: alt text describes **what is in the frame**, specifically, in one
sentence, from a single registry (`lib/photos.ts`) that sits next to the file
path. No keyword stuffing, no "image of", no decorative placeholders. 288/288
compliant. New photos must be added to the registry with alt text or the
compiler will not have a key to reference.

## 6. Google Search Console readiness plan

**Deploy first.** None of this can be done from the codebase.

1. **Verify.** `public/google807aab8c24a997b5.html` is in place and ships at
   `https://raincitypms.com/google807aab8c24a997b5.html`. Add the property as a
   **Domain property** if DNS access exists (covers www/non-www and http/https
   in one); otherwise URL-prefix with the HTML file.
2. **Submit** `https://raincitypms.com/sitemap.xml`. Expect **28 URLs
   submitted** after the Phase-1 sitemap change (36 today, minus the 8 noindex
   blog URLs that are being removed from it).
3. **Request indexing** on the 5 core pages, in this order: `/`, `/services`,
   `/locations`, `/contact`, `/about`. Then the 11 service pages. Then the 9
   location pages. Roughly 10/day — the quota is per-property and daily.
4. **Coverage checks, day 3–7:**
   - "Excluded by 'noindex' tag" — should list only `/terms` and
     `/privacy-policy`. If blog URLs still appear, the sitemap change did not
     deploy.
   - "Page with redirect" — should list only `/disclaimer` and
     `/refund-policy`. Expected and correct.
   - "Discovered – currently not indexed" — normal on a new property. If a
     location page is still there at day 21, it needs more internal links or
     more depth.
   - **"Duplicate without user-selected canonical" — the one to watch.** If it
     ever names location pages, the doorway risk has materialised. It should
     not; measured overlap is 24%.
5. **Enhancements tab:** FAQ, Breadcrumb and Merchant/LocalBusiness reports
   should populate within 1–2 weeks. Zero FAQ items after 2 weeks means the
   JSON-LD did not deploy — validate at
   `search.google.com/test/rich-results`.
6. **Mobile usability / Core Web Vitals:** insufficient field data is normal
   below a traffic threshold. Use PageSpeed Insights lab data in the interim;
   re-check after the webp conversion.
7. **Bing Webmaster Tools:** import the GSC property. Five minutes, and it also
   feeds ChatGPT's search index — which is a GEO channel, not just a Bing one.

worth more than everything else in this document combined. It is also the one

**Website side — done or doing:**

- `sameAs` once social URLs arrive. Blocked on client.

**Client side — must be done by RainCity, in priority order:**

1. **Claim/verify the Google Business Profile.** Set it as a *service-area

5. **Photos.** Same set as the 8 outstanding service-tile shots — one shoot

## 8. AEO / GEO — answer and generative engine optimization

This site is already unusually well positioned here, and it is worth being
precise about why rather than adding tactics for their own sake.

**Already working:**

- **111 question-and-answer pairs**, marked up as `FAQPage` on all 20 service
  and location pages. Answers are written as complete, self-contained
  paragraphs — extractable without the surrounding page, which is exactly what
  an answer engine needs.
- **`llms.txt`** at the site root, with every service and community URL and a
  plain-language description of the business.
- **`robots.txt` names 13 AI crawlers explicitly** (GPTBot, ClaudeBot,
  PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended and others) rather than
  leaving them to the wildcard — the difference between "not blocked" and
  "invited."
- **Entity clarity:** one `Organization` node with a stable `@id`, referenced
  rather than re-declared on every page. Answer engines resolve entities before
  they resolve pages.

**To add:**

- **`WebSite` node** — completes the entity graph (Organization → WebSite →
  WebPage). Phase 1.
- **`llms.txt` refresh** — must be updated in the same pass as any content
  change. It is the one file that does not derive from `content.ts`.
- **Direct-answer discipline in all future FAQ copy:** the answer's *first
  sentence* must answer the question completely. Everything after it is
  context. Several existing answers open with context and answer in sentence
  two — fine for a reader, worse for an extractor. This is a rule for new copy,
  not a rewrite mandate for good existing copy.
- **The blog is the biggest GEO gap and it is switched off.** Informational
  queries are what answer engines cite most. Six `noindex` articles is six
  citations not happening. This is the same blocker as everywhere else in this
  document.

**Explicitly not doing:** `Speakable` markup (perpetual limited pilot),
`HowTo` schema (the process blocks are 3-step summaries, not instructions —
marking them up would misrepresent them), and any AI-content-detection
gaming.

## 10. Success metrics and honest timeline

Written to be forwarded to the client as-is.

### First 14 days — what should actually happen

These are *indexing* signals, not ranking signals. Anyone promising rankings in
14 days is selling something.

| Signal | Expected | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Property verified | Day 1 | GSC |
| Sitemap read, 28 URLs discovered | Day 1–3 | GSC → Sitemaps |
| 20–28 URLs indexed | Day 7–14 | GSC → Pages |
| Impressions appear, mostly brand + long-tail | Day 7–14 | GSC → Performance |
| FAQ + Breadcrumb enhancements populate | Day 7–14 | GSC → Enhancements |
| Crawl requests rise then settle | Day 3–14 | GSC → Crawl stats |
| Clicks | **Low single digits. This is normal.** | GSC → Performance |

**A realistic day-14 statement:** *"The site is fully indexed, Google is
reading all our structured data, and we're appearing in search results for
long-tail and brand queries. Competitive rankings come next."*

### 1–3 months

- Long-tail and low-competition local terms start ranking (page 2–3 moving to
  page 1) — e.g. "gutter cleaning Anmore," "pressure washing New Westminster."
- Location pages begin ranking for their city + service terms.
- First organic quote-form submissions.
- Google Business Profile — if claimed and gathering reviews — starts producing
  local-pack impressions, which will likely exceed organic in this sector.

### 3–6 months

- Competitive terms ("pressure washing Surrey," "gutter cleaning Burnaby")
  reach page 1 **if** the review gap is closed and the blog is live.
- Blog posts start earning informational impressions and AI-answer citations —
  **only if the `noindex` is lifted.**
- Enough data to decide on the Phase-7 matrix from evidence rather than guess.

### What will hold this back, plainly

1. **Six blog articles are invisible to Google by choice.** Until they are
   confirmed and the `noindex` comes off, the entire informational funnel — the
   part that feeds AI answer engines — is off.
2. **Zero published reviews while every competitor shows hundreds.** No amount
   of on-page work compensates for that in local search.
3. **The Google Business Profile.** For a service-area business this is
   plausibly worth more than the whole website. If it is unclaimed, that is the
   first thing to fix on Monday.

None of the three is a code problem. All three are the client's to unblock.
